Miami in 72 Hours

A tour-ready sequence that keeps beach, culture, art, and water in sensible geographic order—without spending the whole trip in traffic.

Use it this way: keep each day in one main zone. Book the anchor experience first, then place food and flexible stops around it.

Your three-day route

Swap days for weather, but avoid mixing distant neighborhoods on the same day.

Arrival rules

  • MIA: allow 45–90 minutes to Miami Beach depending on traffic and pickup.
  • PortMiami: do not schedule a timed tour immediately after disembarkation.
  • Rental car: often unnecessary if staying in Miami Beach and using tours.
  • Flight day: keep the last activity on the mainland when practical.

Booking order

  1. Book the Everglades or boat anchor.
  2. Reserve Little Havana food/culture experience.
  3. Add flexible beach and neighborhood stops.
  4. Recheck pickup location and cancellation deadline.
  5. Screenshot confirmations and meeting points.
