7 Best Boat Tours in Miami Right Now (2026 Local's Guide)

If you're searching boat tours in Miami, you get 200+ options and half of them are the same crowded party boat. After looking at reviews, routes, and what actually sells out, here are the 7 that are worth your money right now.

1. Sunset Cruise - Biscayne Bay & South Beach

This is the #1 seller for a reason. You get the skyline, the light is perfect for photos, and it's cooler than the mid-day tours. Go for the 2-hour sailing version, not the 90-minute speedboat if you want to relax.

Best for: Couples, first-time visitors, photos
Local tip: Book the 6:30pm - 7:30pm slot in summer. Sun sets after 8pm, you get golden hour the whole time.

2. Millionaire's Row & Celebrity Homes Cruise

Yes, it's touristy. Yes, you still want to do it. It's 90 minutes, cheap, and you see Star Island, Fisher Island, and all the houses you see on Instagram.

Best for: Families, short on time
Avoid: The boats that say "party cruise" for this route - you can't hear the guide.

3. Everglades + Biscayne Bay Combo

If you only have one day, this is the smartest buy. Airboat in the morning when gators are active, bay cruise in the afternoon. Saves you driving.

Check Combo Tour Availability →

4. Speedboat Thrill - 45min Adrenaline Run

Not a sightseeing tour, it's a rollercoaster on water. Loud, fast, you will get wet. Don't bring a hat.

Best for: Bachelor/bachelorette, teens

5. Private Sailing Charter (Up to 6 People)

For $400-$600 split between friends, it's cheaper per person than you think and you skip the 40-person crowd. Best value if you have 4+ people.

6. Night Lights & City Lights Cruise

Underrated. Miami at night from the water is better than day. Less crowded, cooler, skyline is lit.

7. Snorkeling + Boat Tour - Key Biscayne

If you want to actually get IN the water, not just look at it. Water is clearest in the morning.

How to Pick: Quick Guide

  • Want photos? -> Sunset Cruise #1 or #6
  • On a budget / short on time? -> #2 Millionaire's Row
  • Want nature? -> #3 Combo
  • Want party? -> #4 Speedboat

All tours above depart from Bayside Marketplace or Miami Beach Marina. Parking at Bayside is $20-$30, take an Uber if you can.

Local Tips to Avoid Getting Burned

  1. Morning is better for Everglades, evening is better for Biscayne Bay. Don't flip it.
  2. If it says "free drinks" and is under $40, it's a booze cruise first, tour second.
  3. Book 2-3 days ahead in summer, 7 days ahead for weekends.
