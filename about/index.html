EDITORIAL STANDARDS

How Miami tour recommendations are researched

A public methodology for separating useful travel decisions from promotional claims.

Last reviewed August 26, 2026

Our standard: Every recommendation must help a traveler make a decision beyond clicking a marketplace listing. Pages identify their evidence type, reviewed date, practical selection criteria, official sources, affiliate relationship, and details that still require provider confirmation.

What this site is

Miami's Best Tours is an independent editorial and affiliate travel site. It publishes Miami tour comparisons, decision guides, and optional self-service planning products. It is not the City of Miami, a tourism authority, Viator, or a tour operator.

The Miami's Best Tours Editorial Team is accountable for the site's guide research and corrections.

Our five-part comparison framework

CriterionWhat is checkedWhy it matters
Traveler fitPace, physical demands, group atmosphere, age rules, and accessibility information.A highly rated tour can still be wrong for a specific group.
Route logicMeeting area, transfers, check-in, total clock time, and how the outing connects to the rest of the day.Miami geography can turn a short activity into a long logistics problem.
Experience clarityVessel or vehicle, route, inclusions, narration, stops, restrictions, and what promotional terms actually mean.Similar titles can describe materially different experiences.
Evidence strengthOfficial sources, current provider information, rating context, review volume, and documented first-hand evidence when available.Claims should be traceable and current.
Booking riskCancellation terms, weather dependence, pickup rules, accessibility gaps, and details requiring confirmation.The best choice protects the traveler's time and money.

How evidence is labeled

Provider-researched means the page compares current operator or marketplace information with relevant official sources. Editorial comparison means products or formats are evaluated against the framework above. First-hand appears only when the page names who attended, the date, and the original observations or images collected.

The absence of a first-hand label is deliberate. Research-based guidance can still be useful, but the site will not imply personal attendance that it cannot document.

Product selection and affiliate independence

Affiliate availability does not guarantee inclusion or favorable placement. Products may be removed for unclear logistics, poor traveler fit, missing practical information, or insufficient value. Commission size is not a ranking criterion. Prices, ratings, inventory, operator terms, and cancellation rules remain live variables and must be verified before booking.

Read the complete affiliate disclosure.

Sources, review dates, and corrections

Official and first-party sources are preferred for park access, port rules, transportation, weather, safety, and destination logistics. Provider pages are used for product-specific details. A reviewed date means the material decision factors and linked sources were checked; it does not guarantee that every price or departure remains unchanged afterward.

To flag an error, call (305) 250-1202 and identify the page, passage, and supporting source. Material corrections receive a new reviewed date.
