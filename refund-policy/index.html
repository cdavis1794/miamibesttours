Digital-product refund policy

The exact scope of the 7-day digital-product fit promise.

Effective August 5, 2026

Our 7-day fit promise

If Miami in 72 Hours or Miami Trip Command Center materially differs from its description or you cannot access it, request help within seven days of purchase using the support form inside the product. We will restore access, correct the material issue, or refund the digital product.

What is not covered

Changed travel plans, a provider's price or availability, or dissatisfaction with a third-party booking do not establish that the digital product was misdescribed. We do not refund tours or other provider purchases; the provider's terms apply.

Request details

Include the purchase email, product name, and a clear description of the issue. Approved refunds return through the original payment method. The financial institution controls when the credit appears.
